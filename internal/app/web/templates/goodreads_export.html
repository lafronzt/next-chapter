Before you upload

  • Goodreads needs an ISBN for every row, and the book must exist in its catalog. Books without a usable ISBN are listed below and left out.
  • Every row names its shelf: read, currently-reading or to-read. Goodreads keeps the shelf a book is already on, so re-uploading does not move existing books.
  • Ratings and reviews in the file can overwrite the ones already on Goodreads. What a blank value does there is not confirmed, so check a few books after uploading.
  • Ratings are exported as you gave them, including quarter stars.
  • Date Read comes from your newest reading of a book on the read shelf, and only when it is a full date. Date Added, publisher, binding, average rating, and tags are left blank.
  • Private notes and “why I want to read this” are never exported. The order of your queue is not affected.
