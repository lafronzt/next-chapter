What gets shared

  • Nothing leaves this server until you press a button for an AI suggestion and confirm. Nothing is sent in the background, on a schedule, or when a page loads.
  • Each request shows exactly which books and fields it sends before you confirm: titles, authors, tags, page counts, series, want-to-read, your ratings and estimated ratings, finish dates, whether a book is borrowed or ready for pickup and how many days remain, and any note you write for the AI.
  • Never sent: library card or loan account details, your password or sessions, ISBNs, Goodreads IDs, or review text (unless you tick a box for that one request).
  • {{if .AI.Endpoint}}Whoever runs the AI server can see what you send.{{else}}The provider’s own terms apply to what you send.{{end}} Answers are suggestions: you preview them, and nothing changes until you apply one.
