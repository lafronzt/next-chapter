Checks up to 5 unlinked books per run. It sends only each book’s ISBN, title, and author, and only when you press the button. You confirm every link on the next page.

Import your library

Bring your Hardcover shelves, ratings, and finish dates into this library. You preview everything first; new books wait at the end of your queue marked Needs placement, and your order never changes.

{{template "fields" .}}

Reads only your own library (needs the read:library scope) and costs about one request per 100 books. Reviews, notes, and lists are never imported. Requires a tested connection.

Send to Hardcover

Send the shelf status and rating of books you have linked. You preview what Hardcover has and what would change first, and nothing is sent on its own: not after an import, and not when you reorder, finish, or rate a book here. Books where Hardcover is ahead are shown as conflicts and are not sent.

{{template "fields" .}}

Needs the write:library scope. Only Hardcover’s book id, status, rating, and, for a new row, a privacy setting ({{.PrivacyName}} unless you change it in Preferences) are sent. Up to 50 linked books per preview.

Forget Hardcover ids

Clears every Hardcover id kept here: book links, the memory of deleted books’ ids, sync history, cached catalog data, and the ties between readings and Hardcover reads. Every book, rating, and reading stays, and Hardcover is not contacted. You see the counts first.

{{template "fields" .}}

Disconnect

Removes the local account link, test history, and sync history, and turns off requests. Keeps every book and its Hardcover link and leaves Hardcover untouched. To revoke the token, visit your Hardcover API settings and remove it from the server environment.
